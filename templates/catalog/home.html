How it works

  1. 1

    Join a small group

    Each program runs with up to twelve people, on the same evening every week, for eight weeks.

  2. 2

    Meet on Zoom

    Your Zoom link waits in your account and opens fifteen minutes before each session.

  3. 3

    Keep going in between

    A short exercise each week, Elena's notes, and the recording if you missed an evening. The thread fills in as you go.
